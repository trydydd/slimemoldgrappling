Each belt level includes a club-defined technical requirement and a student-chosen personal development pathway. Students must complete the technical requirement and select **three out of four** developmental domains. Within each chosen domain, the student selects **1–2 goals** to work toward over the course of their current belt.

---

## White → Blue Belt

### Technical Requirements (Club-Defined)
- Insert skill expectations here

### Self-Regulation & Awareness (Choose one option)
- Recognize moments of dysregulation and take action to reset  
- Take a break or modify training without shame or apology  
- Express boundaries with partners in live training  

### Conflict & Communication (Choose one option)
- Use a respectful feedback model post-roll  
- Give and receive basic verbal feedback  
- Participate in a repair conversation after a miscommunication or rough round  

### Community Contribution (Choose one option)
- Welcome new students and help orient them to the room  
- Offer to partner with a lower experience partner  
- Participate in at least one safety-related action (e.g. speaking up, stepping in)  

### Growth Mindset & Reflection (Choose one option)
- Log and reflect on at least 3 training setbacks or "failures"  
- Set and track one process-based training goal  
- Celebrate a teammate's progress or effort publicly

## Blue → Purple Belt

### Technical Requirements (Club-Defined)
- Insert skill expectations here

### Self-Regulation & Awareness (Choose one option)
- Use journaling or reflection tools to track emotional/mental patterns in training  
- Identify triggers and use regulation strategies to remain present  
- Help co-regulate with a partner during a stressful or intense roll  

### Conflict & Communication (Choose one option)
- Offer detailed feedback in a way that supports learning  
- Initiate a repair after noticing rupture (discomfort, misunderstanding, tension)  
- Ask for and integrate feedback into your own development  

### Community Contribution (Choose one option)
- Help facilitate drills or games for beginners  
- Participate in safety or cultural maintenance (e.g. safety talk, consent reminder)  
- Initiate support for someone after a visibly hard day on the mats  

### Growth Mindset & Reflection (Choose one option)
- Experiment intentionally with discomfort and record lessons learned  
- Reflect on a plateau or regression and share that process with a peer or coach  
- Participate in one peer-led or group reflection session

## Purple → Brown Belt

### Technical Requirements (Club-Defined)
- Insert skill expectations here

### Self-Regulation & Awareness (Choose one option)
- Maintain emotional regulation during high-stakes or chaotic rounds  
- Verbally debrief with partners after intense rolls  
- Help others develop awareness and regulation in training spaces  

### Conflict & Communication (Choose one option)
- Model repair and call-in behavior to resolve tensions in the room  
- Mediate or de-escalate a conflict involving others  
- Facilitate or co-lead a discussion around feedback or community expectations  

### Community Contribution (Choose one option)
- Lead part of a warmup, drill, or recovery session  
- Serve as an anchor for new members or those in vulnerable positions  
- Help onboard a new assistant coach, peer leader, or facilitator  

### Growth Mindset & Reflection (Choose one option)
- Share your grappling learning journey with newer members  
- Publish or present a short reflection, game, or concept to the team  
- Help a peer through a plateau or confidence struggle

## Brown → Black Belt

### Technical Requirements (Club-Defined)
- Insert skill expectations here 

### Self-Regulation & Awareness (Choose one option)
- Demonstrates high-level awareness of personal triggers, defaults, and patterns  
- Coaches regulation and presence in others without force or coercion  
- Reflects on emotional labor and seeks sustainability as a leader  

### Conflict & Communication (Choose one option)
- Supports others in working through conflict and returning to connection  
- Models a long-term feedback loop: gives, receives, integrates, returns  
- Helps co-design systems that make communication and repair easier across the club  

### Community Contribution (Choose one option)
- Actively develops other leaders in the space  
- Initiates or maintains long-term projects that support access, safety, or inclusion  
- Coaches others in navigating group dynamics or difficult interpersonal moments  

### Growth Mindset & Reflection (Choose one option)
- Models and mentors through ongoing discomfort and uncertainty  
- Reflects publicly on failure, doubt, or identity evolution within the sport  
- Develops learning environments that help others do the same

Have a game that works at your gym? Share it — no account, no GitHub, no markdown required. Fill in the form below and your game goes straight into our review queue; once a maintainer approves it, it appears in the [games library]({{% relref "/games/_index.md" %}}).

A good game submission usually has:

* a clear **position** both players start from,
* one focused **objective** per player (what they're trying to learn or do),
* **constraints** that keep the game on-task, and
* a **win condition** so rounds can reset quickly.

New games are tagged `untested` until someone has run them in class. If you'd like credit, leave your name and we'll include it in the game's attribution.
